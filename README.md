# Ocayur Admin - Ayurveda Lifestyle

Modern, responsive administration and management portal for **Ocayur - Ayurveda Lifestyle**.

## Features & Modules
- **Login / Authentication**: Secure credential validation with dual session management.
- **Dashboard**: High-level clinic analytics, patient visit charts, and operational metrics.
- **Appointments Management**: Interactive booking schedule, reassigning doctors, status updates.
- **Patients Directory**: Comprehensive clinical patient records, contact directories, and filters.
- **Doctors & Practitioners**: Doctor profiles, specializations, consultation scheduling, and status toggles.
- **Ayurvedic Packages**: Wellness package administration, pricing, duration, and descriptions.
- **Workshops**: Seminar schedules, registration tracking, online/hybrid meeting coordination.

## Brand Identity & Aesthetic
- **Logo**: Extracted high-resolution vector and transparent PNG assets with the botanical olive green logotype, terracotta stethoscope accent arch, and "Ayurveda Lifestyle" tagline.
- **Typography**: Editorial serif headers powered by `Cormorant Garamond` paired with high-legibility geometric sans `Plus Jakarta Sans`.
- **Palette**: Botanical Forest Green (`#2C3E2D`), Warm Terracotta Spice (`#944B27`), Warm Linen Canvas (`#F8F7F4`), Crisp Card Surface (`#FFFFFF`).
